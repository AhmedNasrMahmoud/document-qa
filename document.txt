
    Acme Corp Refund Policy
    
    Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
    After 30 days, only store credit is available.
    Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
    To request a refund, email support@acme.com with your order number.
    
    Acme Corp Shipping Policy
    
    Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs $5.99.
    Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.
    Free shipping is available on orders over $50.
    We ship to all 50 US states and Canada.
    
    Acme Corp Return Policy
    
    Items must be returned in original packaging.
    Electronics cannot be returned after 15 days.
    Clothing items can be returned within 60 days with tags attached.
